Assistant

Help when you ask for it. Quiet when you don't.

The assistant sits in a side panel and knows your grid, your crossings and which checks are failing. It suggests; you decide what goes in.

FILL Finds words that fit your crossings, like C?U?E.
CLUES Drafts clues in house style, from Monday-easy to Saturday-tricky.
RULES Explains why a check fails and which squares to move.
STYLE Flags clues that repeat their answer or break house style.
